How to use it

Three inputs, one dish. It takes about two seconds.

1

List what you actually have Plain words are fine — "half a cabbage, some paneer, two tomatoes". Rough quantities help but are not required. In a hurry? Click one of the example fridges instead, or hit ↻ for different ones.

2

Set your time and diet The time budget genuinely changes the recipe, not just the label. Ten minutes gets you assembly; forty gets you something simmered.

3

Cook something You get a named dish, four to six steps, and — if it needs anything you did not list — an honest "missing" list plus substitutions.
